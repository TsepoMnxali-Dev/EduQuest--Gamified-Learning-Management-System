Frequently Asked Questions

How do quizzes work?

Each attempt gives you 10 random questions from the quiz, and the time limit is shown at the top. You must answer every question before you can submit. When you submit, you see your score and a review of each question with the correct answer and an explanation.

Can I retake a quiz?

Yes. Open the subject on the Classes page and choose Retake Quiz. Your Quizzes page always shows your most recent result, and you can review it at any time.

Why does a quiz say it is not available?

A quiz appears as not available until it has at least 10 approved questions. It also has to be published for your grade and one of your subjects. Check back soon, or send us a message below if it stays that way.

How are points and rankings worked out?

You earn 10 points for each correct answer. Only your best attempt on each quiz counts, so retaking a quiz can improve your score but cannot be used to collect extra points. The leaderboard has This Week, This Month and All Time views, and other learners appear as a first name and surname initial.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the Sign In page, choose Forgot password and enter your email address. We will send you a link to choose a new password. The link works once and expires after 1 hour.

Why can I only see some subjects?

Your Classes and Quizzes show only the subjects you chose when you signed up, for your grade.

How do I change my subjects, grade or school?

This cannot be changed on your own yet. Send us a message below with the change you need, and we will update it for you.

I found a mistake in a question. What should I do?

Thank you for telling us! Send a message below, choose the Quizzes category, and include the quiz name and the question number so we can fix it.
