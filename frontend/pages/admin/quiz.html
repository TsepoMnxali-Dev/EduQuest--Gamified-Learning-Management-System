Maths: write LaTeX between dollar signs, e.g. $\frac{x^2+1}{2}$ or $\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$. This works in the question, every answer option and the explanation, and is what the AI generator uses. Text with no dollar signs at all is typeset as a best guess (x^2, a/b, sqrt(x+1)); once a text has $…$, only what is inside the dollar signs is typeset.
